Faculty control

2–3 October 2026

Held in this tab only. Never saved to this device.

 

Reset one student

For a machine that died mid-paper. It deletes their attempt and their passcode record so they can sit it again. You will be shown who and what before anything goes.

Give a student a new password

For a lost slip, or a password they chose and forgot. The new one is shown once, here, for you to read out. It cannot be looked up again afterwards.

Opening a paper lets anyone with the passcode start it. Publishing a report opens the full answer key and every worked method to everyone who has already sat that paper — so it is pressed once, after the last person has submitted.
